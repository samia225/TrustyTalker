# TrustyTalker: project summary

**Problem.** JARA is MCRC Health's research-stage chatbot for adults with substance use and mental health concerns. Some replies sound supportive but miss what the person needs. A reply might encourage someone to quit when they only asked for information, overlook an earlier preference, state something its sources do not support, or miss signs of risk. Reviewers need a dependable way to find these replies.

**Approach.** TrustyTalker is a review tool that runs offline in any web browser. It follows each person's goal, readiness and preferences through the conversation and applies 18 rule-based checks to JARA's replies, covering areas such as goal alignment, drift, source support, crisis response and unsafe advice. Each finding quotes the reply, rates its severity as critical or quality, and suggests where the fix belongs. An optional AI judge looks for subtler problems, and a human reviewer makes the final decision on every finding.

**Results.** We created 28 fictional cases (59 conversations), including three scenarios written and rated by MCRC, and labelled them before running the checks. A second team member reviewed 12 conversations independently and agreed with every label, and MCRC also reviewed the labels. The checks agree with our answer key on 57 of 59 conversations (97%). They identify 42 of 44 problem replies, including all 17 critical ones, with no false alarms. Only the AI judge flagged the two mildly pushy replies the rules missed. These are development results, since the team saw every case. Clinician review is the next step.
